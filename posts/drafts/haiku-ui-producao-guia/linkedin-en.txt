A small model with the right memory beat the bigger model without it. At 8% of the price.

I ran 2,640 calls to find out how much a UX data lake helps Haiku review production screens when the request is vague ("get this ready to ship").

What showed up:

→ No rules: Haiku got 46%.
→ With 25%, 50% and 100% of the rules: each step added 8 to 12 points, up to 70%.
→ With the rules and a verifier alongside: 78%.
→ Sonnet, the bigger model, without the rules: 61%.

And it was not just text volume. Giving the same amount of rules from other subjects only moved it from 46% to 51%. The right rule is what counts.

The caveat I am not hiding: the screens are synthetic and the task is reviewing a draft, not creating from scratch.
