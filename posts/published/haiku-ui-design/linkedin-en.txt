Can a small model with open rules and minimal code get close to a bigger model? I measured it, pre-registered, with open data.

Full study: Haiku 5.5 with a "kit" (30 open-source UX rules + a code checker) against plain Sonnet 5.5, on three login-screen tasks. 2,244 items, 2,424 calls, 0 failures at the end.

The numbers:

→ Create: from 0.744 to 0.998 with the kit.
→ Improve: from 0.754 to 0.926.
→ Haiku with the kit reached 1.07 times Sonnet's combined accuracy, at 10% of the cost per task.

And what the article does not hide:

• Comparing two screens, the kit did not help and made it slightly worse.
• Three human-free aesthetics instruments were validated and none passed: the study claims nothing about beauty.
• The biggest threat to validity is circular: the 30 rules the kit checks are the same ones that score create and improve. An independent axe-core check confirms the gain on create, but not on improve.

https://melolabs.net/en/haiku-ui-design/
